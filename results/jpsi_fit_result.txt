Analysis Summary

The invariant mass distribution of opposite-charge muon pairs
was analyzed in the J/psi mass region from 2.9 to 3.3 GeV.

A Gaussian function was fitted to the reconstructed mass peak.
The estimated peak position was 3.0920 ± 0.0046 GeV, which is
close to the expected J/psi mass of approximately 3.0970 GeV.

The difference between the reconstructed and expected mass may
be influenced by limited statistics, background events, histogram
binning, and the use of a Gaussian-only model without an explicit
background component.
